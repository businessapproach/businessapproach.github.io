How we check prices

Every number on our posts and guides follows the same rules.

  1. Real store pages only. Each starter-kit price comes from a current product page at a US retailer (like Home Depot, Walmart, Target or Best Buy) or an official site. Search snippets alone don't count.
  2. Rounded honestly. We round to a simple estimate with a tilde (~$13) and never round down to make an idea look cheaper.
  3. "Free" means free. We only say free when the tool or plan is free to start, and we mention any fee taken when you get paid.
  4. Sources listed. Every guide ends with the store, product and link we used, plus the date we checked.
  5. Customer prices are cited. "What customers pay" ranges come from published cost guides (like Angi or HomeGuide). If we can't find a reliable one, we say so instead of guessing.
  6. Prices change. Sales end and stock runs out. Treat our numbers as a starting point and check before you buy.
