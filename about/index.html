About Business Approach

Legit, low-cost business ideas. Researched, priced and explained.

Business Approach started as an Instagram and Facebook page sharing one simple post a day: a handful of small businesses you can start for a clear, honest budget. This site is where each of those posts gets the full story.

What we believe

  • Only legit, doable ideas. Legal, proven work that pays. No MLMs, no get-rich-quick schemes, nothing that needs savings, a big following or luck.
  • Budget-honest. If a post says "under $100", the starter kit really costs under $100, and we show you where we found each price.
  • No earnings promises. We share what customers typically pay professionals, from published cost guides. What you earn depends on your area, your effort and your prices.
  • Check your local rules. Licenses, permits and insurance vary by city and state. We point you to them; we can't give legal or tax advice.
